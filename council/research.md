# Novelty pass — prior art (searched 31 Oct 2026, ~2:00 PM IST)

| Idea | Prior art | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting → summary + action items | Asthana et al., "Summaries, Highlights, and Action Items" (Microsoft; arXiv 2307.15793; PACM HCI 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15793, https://doi.org/10.1145/3711074). Also commercial: Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Granola, Wispr Notetaker itself | **Done.** Commodity; no credit for this alone |
| Cross-meeting decision memory with supersession links | Zep/Graphiti temporal KG — LLM compares new facts to related facts and invalidates contradicted edges, keeps history (Rasmussen et al., arXiv 2501.13956, https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13956; https://github.com/getzep/graphiti). Open-source "verbatim" (https://github.com/qatcod/verbatim) does extraction + reconciliation + HITL confirm + push to Linear/GitHub. Blog pattern: https://acta.ai/blogs/multi-meeting-rag-decisions-and-commitments | **Technique exists** as infra/dev tools. Novel part for us is the *product surface*: a conflict raised *at the moment of speaking*, framed as an objection with both quotes, plus a human verdict (overrule/keep/flag) |
| Distinguishing contradiction vs refinement vs duplicate | Graphiti dedupe/contradiction prompt (graphiti_core/prompts/dedupe_edges.py) | Reuse the *idea* (classes: new / confirms / changes / reverses); measuring false positives on our own eval set is our proof |
| Cleaning disfluent speech (fillers, self-corrections) into structured output | LLM disfluency removal (STIL 2024, https://aclanthology.org/2024.stil-1.16.pdf); GenSEC post-ASR challenge (arXiv 2409.09785, https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09785); CorrectionLM for dialogue-state tracking (arXiv 2410.18209) | **Known to work.** Novel twist: self-corrections inside a debrief ("30th, actually no 28th") resolved AND shown as a visible "you corrected yourself" trace |
| Knowledge tracing + misconception detection from explanations (statement 02) | LLMKT (Scarlatos, Baker, Lan, arXiv 2409.16490, LAK 2025); LLM gap detection in code explanations (arXiv 2501.10365; notes 27–34% hallucinated gaps); MAP math misconception benchmark (ACL AIMECON 2025, https://aclanthology.org/2025.aimecon-wip.3.pdf); LLM on physics explanations, kappa 0.74 vs humans (arXiv 2508.14823) | Research-backed and *spoken* reasoning is fresh, but hallucination risk is documented → proof is hard in 9h |
| Finance (04) and career (05) | Not searched in depth; crowded consumer apps (Fi, Jupiter, CRED; Teal, Huntr, Final Round AI) | Low novelty |

**Takeaway:** nothing here is "never done" in research. Hackathon-level novelty = (a) speech-native capture of decisions in the 30 s after a call, (b) conflict-at-capture with provenance, (c) a measured precision/recall panel incl. false-positive (refinement ≠ conflict) cases. Claim "built on the temporal-memory idea from Zep (2025)", not "first ever".
